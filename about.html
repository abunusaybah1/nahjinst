An-Nahj Islamic Institute

Founded in early 2025, An-Nahj Islamic Institute was established with a clear mission: to spread beneficial knowledge until it reaches everyone, by the permission of Allah.

The institute provides a structured, reliable, and accessible platform where Muslims can learn the Qur’an, Arabic, Hadith, and basic Fiqh (Islamic jurisprudence) from anywhere.

What Makes An-Nahj Different?

Affordable

Commitment fees are pocket friendly, making it very much affordable. We believe that beneficial knowledge should be accessible to all, regardless of their financial status.

Flexible

Classes run in the evenings/nights, from Maghrib downwards, perfect for students and workers. We also offer a variety of personalized learning options to suit your availability.

Sound Manhaj

Curriculum grounded in the Qur'an and Sunnah upon the way of the pious predecessors. Knowledge is the key to understanding Islam, and must be sought inline with the methodology of the pious predecessors.

Fully Online

Learn from anywhere in the world via the digital tools you are already familiar with. Being far from a physical location is no longer a barrier to accessing Islamic quality education.
