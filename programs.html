Our Programs and Classes

At An-Nahj Islamic Institute, our structured programs are designed to help students learn the Qur’an, Arabic, Hadith, and Fiqh with clarity and discipline.


Every student belongs to a class. For every class, all the activities listed below are compulsory for all students in that class. Any student who fails to participate in any of them (even if it is just one) will be penalized for inactivity.

Abubakar Class

Qur’an memorization, revision, and Tajweed for learners of all levels.

Class activities:

  • Qur’an memorization
  • Tajweed
  • Hadith
  • Murāja'ah
  • Fiqh

Umar Class

Arabic Reading Course - learn Arabic letters, pronunciation, letter joining, and how to read the Qur’an from scratch.

Class activities:

  • Lessons from Al-Bidāyah
  • Hadith
  • Fiqh

Uthman Class

Arabic Language and Speaking Course - Learn Arabic, Nahw, Sarf, and Ta'beer.

Class activities:

  • Arabiyyah
  • Hadith
  • Nahw
  • Sarf
  • Ta'beer
  • Fiqh

Additional Information

Class Timing

All classes take place in the evenings/nights, from after Maghrib, making them suitable for students, workers, and busy adults.


Some classes are conducted one-on-one while others are conducted in groups. All details will be communicated as due, in shā' Allāh.

Teaching Methodology

All our classes are conducted on WhatsApp and Telegram. All required materials will be shared with students throughout the program.

All classes are taken in Arabic, English, and Yoruba language, with adequate explanation.

Fees and Enrollment

Inline with our commitment to make quality Islamic education affordable to everyone, we make use of commitment fees at the institute.

Commitment fees are expected to be paid at the beginning of each month, with a grace period of the first 10 days to make things easier for everyone.

Any payment that will not be made on or before the 10th day of the month must be communicated to the director without delay.

Enrollment remains open throughout the year, and students can join at any time, bi idhniLlāhi.
